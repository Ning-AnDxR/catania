Hand-drawn ink and pale watercolour sea chart for a board game, in the manner of a
19th-century nautical chart or copper-plate book engraving.

Style: fine ink line work with hatching and stipple for texture, never gradients or colour
blocks. Muted Morandi sea tones only: pale grey-teal #9EB6BA, mid grey-teal #75919B, deep
grey-teal #5D7B85, and a little aged parchment #E8E2D0. NO black ink - the darkest line in the
picture is a soft grey-teal. Flat, even, very low contrast: this image is a background layer
that a game board gets drawn on top of, so it must never compete with it.

Subject: open water on an old chart. Irregular short rows of fine wave hatching spread evenly
across the whole sheet; a handful of long gentle current lines curving across it; faint rhumb
lines radiating from the middle of the sheet out towards the edges. A small empty compass rose
drawn in line only, placed at the middle of the left edge. A tiny three-masted sailing ship
drawn in line only, at the middle of the right edge. Two or three small bare rock islets with a
scrap of shoreline near the outer edges.

Composition: keep the middle 60% of the sheet almost empty - only very sparse, very faint wave
hatching there - because a hexagonal game board will sit on top of it. All the denser detail
(compass rose, ship, islets, current lines) stays in the outer margin. Flat even light across
the whole sheet, no cast shadow, no vignette, no frame, no drawn border, no ruled grid.

Constraints: no photorealism, no dark or moody lighting, no high-contrast gradient, no 3D
render, no glossy digital painting, no cartoon style, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no sea
monsters, no whales, no land masses in the middle of the sheet, no signature, no watermark.

Size: 2048x1152 landscape.
